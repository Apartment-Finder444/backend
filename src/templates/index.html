      How to read a listing

      Below range
      Asking rent is under the estimated fair rent range.
      Within range
      Asking rent is inside the range. Most listings land here.
      Above range
      Asking rent is over the range, not merely over the middle.
      Not listed
      The listing does not say. That is unknown, not a no.
      No pets allowed
      The listing explicitly says no.

      The fair rent range is an 80% range. Similar listings fell inside it about 8 times in 10 when we tested on listings the model had not seen.
